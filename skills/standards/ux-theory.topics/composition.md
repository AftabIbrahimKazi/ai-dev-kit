# Composition

- **Gestalt:** proximity, similarity, common region, continuity, figure-ground. Use proximity first; add a border or fill (common region) only when proximity fails.
- **Proximity rule:** the gap inside a group is smaller than the gap between groups. Check the ratio is at least 1:2.
- **Hierarchy levers:** size, weight, contrast, position, space, depth. Use 2–3 per distinction; more is noise. One focal point per view. Squint test.
- **Spacing scale:** 4/8pt base (4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96). Every gap and padding comes from the scale as a token.
- **Grid:** 12 columns on desktop, 4 on mobile; gutters from the scale; cap the container (DES-07).
- **Alignment:** one dominant left edge. Align on edges, not centres, for text blocks. Correct optically for round shapes and icons.
- **Balance:** symmetric for formal, asymmetric for dynamic; balance visual weight (size, colour, density), not area.
- **White space:** macro (between sections, 64–96) separates ideas; micro (inside components, 8–16) sets rhythm. Do not fill space because it is empty.
- **Reading paths:** F-pattern for text-heavy pages, Z-pattern for sparse ones. Put the primary action on the path.
- **Targets:** Fitts's law. Touch targets 44px or larger (24px is the WCAG 2.2 minimum); primary actions bigger than secondary.
- **Choice load:** Hick's law. One primary action per view; group the rest.
- **Density:** pick compact, regular or comfortable per theme and hold it.
